Solve the following problem and show all of your reasoning, checking every step twice by a different method. A warehouse has 12 loading docks. Trucks arrive according to a schedule in which truck n arrives at minute 7n mod 97 of each hour and needs (3n mod 11) + 4 minutes at a dock. For n from 1 to 60, determine for every truck which dock it uses if each truck takes the lowest-numbered free dock, how long each truck waits, the longest queue that forms, and the smallest number of docks for which no truck ever waits. Then generalise: derive conditions on the arrival and service formulas under which no queue forms.
