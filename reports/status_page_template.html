__VERDICTS__

Kernel speed

One linear-attention layer (32 heads of 128 × 128). First the complete cycle measured by the test harness, which decides gate G3; then the component timings (decode step plus one sixteenth of a fold, batch 512) that led to the final configuration. bf16 + window is SGLang's 16-bit state with its window kernel, the fastest baseline that keeps accuracy.
